Our point of view

Real estate with less noise.

EstateVista is a fictional brand created to demonstrate a polished, multi-page real-estate website built entirely with front-end technologies.

The idea

Curate first. Explain clearly. Design for decisions.

Many property websites prioritize volume. EstateVista takes the opposite approach: fewer, stronger listings supported by neighborhood context, advisors, saved searches, comparison tools, and clear financial planning.

The brand, properties, people, addresses, imagery, market metrics, and copy are all original fictional demo content intended for a public GitHub portfolio.
